Bitcoin History

Creation of Bitcoin 2008-2009

On 18 August 2008, the domain name bitcoin.org was registered. Later that year, on 31 October, a link to a paper authored by Satoshi Nakamoto titled Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System was posted to a cryptography mailing list.

This paper detailed methods of using a peer-to-peer network to generate what was described as a system for electronic transactions without relying on trust. On 3 January 2009, the bitcoin network came into existence with Satoshi Nakamoto mining the genesis block of bitcoin (block number 0), which had a reward of 50 bitcoins.

The first open source bitcoin client was released on 9 January 2009. One of the first supporters and receivers of the first bitcoin transaction was programmer Hal Finney.

Finney downloaded the bitcoin software the day it was released, and received 10 bitcoins from Nakamoto in the world's first bitcoin transaction on 12 January 2009.

First Bitcoin Transaction

On January 12th, 2009, the network recorded its first transaction. Hal Finney received 10 BTC in a transaction sent by Satoshi Nakamoto.

This was about more than awarding Hal the bragging rights to the first Bitcoin transaction. It was verifiable proof that the network was working as intended. A transfer of value had been recorded on a distributed ledger without reliance on a trusted third party.

Bitcoin's price rise

Bitcoin's price has risen enormously since 2009. It went from being worth almost nothing to becoming a globally recognised digital asset.

Bitcoin Milestones

  • Bitcoin Pizza Day: Laszlo Hanyecz offered to pay 10,000 BTC for two pizzas. It was the first time Bitcoin had been used to buy physical goods.
  • 2009: Bitcoin is first mined in January by Nakamoto. Other miners soon follow.
  • 2011: The value of Bitcoin rises to $1, and by the end of December it reaches more than $13.
  • 2013: Bitcoin's price breaks through $100 and then reaches $200.
  • 2017: Bitcoin reaches an all-time high of nearly $20,000.
  • 2021: El Salvador becomes the first nation to make Bitcoin legal tender.
What's the most important milestone in Bitcoin's history?
The mining of Bitcoin's Genesis Block on January 3, 2009, by Satoshi Nakamoto is widely regarded as one of the most significant moments in Bitcoin's history, as it launched the first decentralized cryptocurrency and established the foundational blockchain.
